Creamy mushroom pasta

A weeknight dinner in 25 minutes: golden mushrooms, garlic and a sauce that coats every ribbon.

25minutes
4servings
520kcal

Ingredients

Servings: 4

Method

Tap a step when it is done.

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water 1 minute less than on the pack.
  2. Fry the sliced mushrooms in oil until golden, 6-7 minutes.
  3. Add garlic for 30 seconds, then the cream, and simmer 3 minutes.
  4. Toss the pasta with the sauce and a splash of pasta water.
  5. Serve with grated parmesan and black pepper.
