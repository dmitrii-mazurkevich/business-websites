Calm living room

Interior · 36 m²2025Tbilisi

A family of four wanted a living room where children can play and adults can read. We kept the walls light, put the storage into one long built-in wall and chose linen and oak that do not mind sticky hands.

The budget stayed within 3% of the plan, the work took nine weeks.
