Rome in three slow days

Rome is easy to rush. Everyone has a list of forty places and two days. I had three days and a rule: no more than two big sights a day and a long lunch between them.

Day one: the old centre on foot

Start early at the Pantheon before the crowds, then walk the side streets to Piazza Navona. The best coffee was in a tiny bar where locals drink it standing in two minutes.

"Slow travel is not about seeing less. It is about remembering more."

Day two: the Colosseum and Monti

Book the first slot of the day, then spend the afternoon in Monti: vintage shops, a market and a dinner that costs half of what it costs near the Forum.
